CLEAR, CONSIDERED, AND YOURS

Privacy & policies.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This page explains how the current Aabha demo works and the store policies shown on this site.

This is a local storefront demo.

Sign-in uses a sample OTP, bag and address details stay in this browser, and checkout does not create an order or collect payment. When an email recipient is configured, the feedback form opens your email app with your details prefilled. It does not send or save a message until you choose to send it.

01 — YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Aabha is currently a browser-based demonstration. It does not send account, bag, or delivery-address details to an Aabha server. The information described below is held by your browser on this device unless a third-party service is specifically noted.

We do not use this demo to run advertising or analytics profiles. Do not enter payment-card details or other sensitive information; no payment service is connected.

02 — WHAT THE DEMO USES

Information and storage

  • Sign-in: the sample sign-in asks for a mobile number and name. It uses the displayed demo OTP 123456; it does not send an SMS. The number and name are kept in this tab's session storage and removed when you sign out or the tab session ends.
  • Bag and delivery: bag contents, saved delivery addresses, and the selected address are stored in this browser's local storage so they remain after a page refresh. They stay on this device until cleared from the site or browser storage.
  • Feedback: the form prepares an email containing your name, mobile number, and message. Your email app sends it to Aabha only if you choose to press Send. The website does not store the message.
  • Admin demo: product and collection edits are saved in this browser's local storage. The manager sign-in state uses tab session storage.

Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to access locally stored information. Clear this site's browser storage to remove saved bag, address, product, and collection data.

03 — EXTERNAL SERVICES

Third-party services

The delivery map uses Leaflet with OpenStreetMap map tiles. When you type an address search or pin a location, the search text or pinned coordinates are sent to Photon, an OpenStreetMap-based geocoder, to return address suggestions and address details such as the PIN code. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap. Google Fonts and external image hosts also provide some fonts and product imagery; your browser contacts those providers to load the assets.

Those providers process requests under their own terms and privacy notices. If you send feedback, your email provider will process the message. This demo has no payment processor or order-management service connected.

04 — YOUR CHOICES

Your choices

You can sign out to clear the demo account details for the current tab, remove saved addresses and bag items in the site, or clear this site's local storage and session storage in your browser settings. If the site is later connected to a real store, its owner will need to provide a working privacy contact for requests about personal information.

05 — USING THE STORE

Store terms

Product descriptions, photographs, colours, availability, and prices are presented for demonstration. Colours may look different across screens. Product and collection changes made in the manager are local to the browser and are not shared with other customers.

The bag and checkout screens are not a purchase offer or order confirmation. The current demo does not accept orders or payments. A live store must publish its verified seller identity, customer-care and grievance contacts, payment methods, and final sale terms before taking orders.

06 — DELIVERY & AFTERCARE

Shipping, returns & refunds

The demo bill currently displays a ₹49 handling charge and a ₹99 delivery charge, with delivery shown as free for orders above ₹2,999. These are display values only; checkout does not arrange shipping or create an order.

Live delivery timelines, cancellation windows, return and exchange eligibility, return-shipping costs, and refund timelines have not been configured. They must be confirmed and displayed by the store owner before live orders are accepted. Nothing on this demo is intended to limit consumer rights under applicable law.

07 — BEFORE LIVE LAUNCH

Store contact details

The business details have not been supplied for this demo. Before accepting orders, add the legal business name, principal business address, customer-care email and phone number, and the grievance officer's name and contact details here. The owner should also replace the demo shipping, cancellation, return, exchange, and refund text with the store's actual terms.
